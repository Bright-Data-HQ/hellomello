# **2\. Term & Commencement**

This engagement commences on 15 May 2026 and runs for a term of six (6) months, concluding on 14 November 2026\.

# **3\. Engagement Overview**

The engagement is structured in two phases. Months 1 and 2 are the build phase, focused on standing up a content-pillar and programmatic-SEO engine. Months 3 to 6 are the scale phase, focused on running the engine, expanding coverage, and maintaining content quality through editorial QA and strategic oversight.

# **4\. Scope of Work**

**4.1 Build Phase: Months 1 & 2**

The biggest organic lever for Hellomello is a content-pillar and programmatic-SEO engine. Pillars give authority, pSEO gives coverage. Deliverables across months 1 and 2:

* **Competitor teardown.** Full map of competitors’ pSEO footprint covering URL patterns, template structure, schema, and internal linking, replicating and improving on the playbook.

* **pSEO template design & development.** Design and development of one well-built pSEO page template that handles location pages, condition pages, and location × condition combinations. Strong on-page SEO, schema, trust signals, a clear conversion path, and fast Core Web Vitals. Included: one template covering the page types above. Additional pSEO templates or significant variants beyond this are out of scope (see Section 8).

* **Data model.** Structured spreadsheet or CMS feeding the template (suburbs, regions, conditions). Built once, reused forever.

* **API integration.** Wiring the data model into the template so pages generate and update from a single source of truth, with content syncing programmatically rather than through manual page-by-page edits.

* **First batch live.** A priority set of pages indexed and interlinked within a few weeks, plus the central pillar page tying them together.

* **Technical fixes.** Audit issues worked through alongside the pillar build, closing conversion and trust leakage on the existing site.

**4.2 Scale Phase: Months 3 to 6**

15 hours per month, heavy involvement on the content side (editorial standards, topic selection, QA on every batch published, and tightening the template as we learn what ranks). Production load gradually shifts to your team. Coverage includes:

* Monthly strategy and review meeting.

* Content QA and editorial guidance on new pillar and cluster batches.

* Core condition hubs (chronic pain, insomnia, stress, endometriosis, PTSD, migraine) plus trust hub and articles launch.

* Expanding the pSEO footprint into adjacent avenues: treatment-type pages (oils, flower, capsules), product comparison pages, eligibility and qualifying-condition pages, cost and Medicare or PBS pages, and FAQ-style long-tail capture. Same template, new datasets, more surface area on Google.

* Regular educational articles published on an ongoing basis, each linking back to the condition hubs to build their search authority. Monthly monitoring on the top pages so we catch any regressions early.

* Wix to headless or custom-CMS migration discovery for FY27.

## Engagement overview

The engagement runs in two phases:

- **Build phase (months 1 and 2):** Stand up a content-pillar and programmatic-SEO (pSEO) engine.
- **Scale phase (months 3 to 6):** Run the engine, expand coverage, and maintain content quality through editorial QA and strategic oversight.

## Build phase deliverables (months 1 and 2)

- **Competitor teardown.** Full map of competitors' pSEO footprint: URL patterns, template structure, schema, and internal linking. Replicate and improve on the playbook.
- **pSEO template design and development.** One well-built pSEO page template covering location pages, condition pages, and location × condition combinations. Strong on-page SEO, schema, trust signals, a clear conversion path, and fast Core Web Vitals. One template only. Additional templates or significant variants are out of scope.
- **Data model.** A structured spreadsheet or CMS feeding the template (suburbs, regions, conditions). Built once, reused forever.
- **API integration.** Wiring the data model into the template so pages generate and update from a single source of truth.
- **First batch live.** A priority set of pages indexed and interlinked within a few weeks, plus the central pillar page tying them together.
- **Technical fixes.** Audit issues worked through alongside the pillar build, closing conversion and trust leakage on the existing site.

## Scale phase coverage (months 3 to 6)

15 hours per month, heavy on the content side: editorial standards, topic selection, QA on every batch published, and tightening the template as the team learns what ranks. Production load gradually shifts to the HM team. Coverage includes:

- Monthly strategy and review meeting.
- Content QA and editorial guidance on new pillar and cluster batches.
- Core condition hubs (chronic pain, insomnia, stress, endometriosis, PTSD, migraine) plus a trust hub and articles launch. (Note: some of these are TGA-restricted, see [04 - Compliance](04-compliance-tga-ahpra.md).)
- Expanding the pSEO footprint into adjacent avenues: treatment-type pages (oils, flower, capsules), product comparison pages, eligibility and qualifying-condition pages, cost and Medicare or PBS pages, and FAQ-style long-tail capture.
- Regular educational articles published on an ongoing basis, each linking back to the condition hubs.
- Monthly monitoring on the top pages to catch regressions early.
- Wix to headless or custom-CMS migration discovery for FY27.

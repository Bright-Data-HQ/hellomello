## The engagement in one paragraph

Bright Data is running a six-month SEO and growth engagement for Hellomello, a national medicinal cannabis telehealth brand. The first two months are a build phase to stand up a content-pillar and programmatic-SEO (pSEO) engine. Months three to six are a scale phase to run the engine, expand coverage, and keep content quality high through editorial QA. Everything stays inside TGA and AHPRA boundaries, and Rod holds final sign-off on all content and website changes.

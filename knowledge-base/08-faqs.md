# 08 - FAQs

Quick answers to the questions that keep coming up. Longer detail lives in the linked docs.

## Is pSEO the best strategy for Hellomello?

Yes, done well. For a national telehealth brand, pSEO is the strongest organic lever, because it is the only way to capture the long tail of location, condition, and intent searches at scale without writing every page by hand. It runs alongside pillar pages, which build authority. Competitors like Herbly prove the template approach can rank; the win comes from going deeper (suburb level) with proper clinical authority. See [03 - Strategy](03-strategy.md).

## Why only 20 suburbs if pSEO is database-fed?

20 is the starting batch, not the limit. The engine is proven on the best 20 suburbs first, using patient data, before pouring in more. A staged rollout protects the site (Google can flag a sudden flood of thin pages) and it is cheaper to fix the template on 20 pages than 2,000. Once batch one indexes, ranks, and converts, the dataset expands to hundreds of suburbs plus conditions, treatment types, and cost or PBS pages.

## Do HM doctors need to sign off YMYL content?

Not right now. Third-party doctors can be the named reviewer to keep things moving, while still giving a credible, attributed reviewer on the page. An HM doctor sign-off later is a bonus and a strong trust signal, but it is not a blocker. See [04 - Compliance](04-compliance-tga-ahpra.md).

## How do we stay inside TGA rules?

Lead with compliant angles: how the service works, practitioner-led location pages, trust and credentials hubs, and general health information. No restricted terms, no product claims. Final publish call stays with Rod. Bright Data provides technical strategy; legal accountability stays with the client.

## Can we rely on competitor traffic numbers?

No, only loosely. Third-party SEO tool figures are roughly correct at best. The reliable signal is that competitor city pages rank for relevant terms and generate traffic, which shows the approach works. Conversion cannot be judged from outside.

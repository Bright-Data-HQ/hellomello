# 01 - Client Profile

## The company

Hellomello (HM) is an Australian telehealth platform for medicinal cannabis. It started about four years ago, inspired by a related hair-loss treatment business. The brand positions itself as a "feel-good healthcare" provider that prioritises accessibility, streamlined automation, and a warm, human-centric service experience rather than a strictly clinical approach.

- **Model:** National telehealth service. There are no physical clinics in each city, which shapes how location-based SEO has to work.
- **Website:** www.hellomello.com.au
- **Tone goal:** Moving towards a more simplified and direct tone of voice, with an updated brand guideline in progress.

## The audience

- **Primary age group:** 35 to 45.
- **Secondary age group:** 25 to 35.
- **Gender split:** Close to even, leaning slightly male at around 55%.

## The team

- **Rod** is the founder and director. He holds final sign-off on all content, website design, and marketing initiatives. This sits with him because of the legal responsibility that comes with being a director, especially given prior regulatory warnings.
- **Alex Ball** is the operations manager. She wears many hats across the CRM, system upgrades, marketing performance, and reporting, and has set up much of the internal reporting.

## Tech stack

- **CRM:** "Cleo", an off-the-shelf, modular CRM that allows more customisation than typical platforms. It integrates with medical record software. Updates currently rely on external developers, which can slow operational improvements.
- **Automation:** The HM team has already done some work with n8n.
- **Website CMS:** Wix today. A migration to headless or a custom CMS is flagged as discovery only for FY27 (see [02 - Engagement Terms](02-engagement-terms.md)).

## Regulatory context (important)

Hellomello operates in medicinal cannabis, which is a heavily regulated space in Australia.

- The company has received prior regulatory warnings, including a TGA warning letter relating to restricted terms (for example chronic pain, anxiety, depression).
- Compliance, legal defence, and final decision-making on content must stay with the client. An agency cannot hold that accountability.
- All work stays inside TGA and AHPRA guardrails. See [04 - Compliance](04-compliance-tga-ahpra.md) for detail.

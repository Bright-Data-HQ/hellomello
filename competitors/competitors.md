# Hellomello — Competitor Landscape

A working overview of competitors in the Australian medicinal cannabis telehealth space, plus adjacent telehealth brands worth watching. Notes capture each competitor's market position, legal/compliance posture, and whether we're comfortable modelling our approach on theirs.

> **Legend — Our position**
> - ✅ **Happy to model** — safe to learn from and emulate
> - ⚠️ **Careful** — compliance risk; review before borrowing tactics
> - 🟡 **Watch / unsure** — monitor before committing

---

## Medicinal cannabis competitors

### Alternaleaf
**Position:** ✅ Happy to model off this
**domain:** alternaleaf.com.au

- The biggest company in the medicinal cannabis space.
- Most funding in the category and has refined their digital presence the most over the past few years.
- Has the largest budget for digital marketing.

**Legal standpoint**
- Currently playing it safe — they're on the regulator's radar.
- Content and copy would have been thoroughly checked by their legal team and should be compliant.
- Used to play more in the grey, but have moved to the safe side.

---

### Herbly
**Position:** 🟡 Happy to model, but want to see if it's effective before going down this path.
**domain:** herbly.com.au

- New player in the space.
- Strong programmatic SEO (pSEO) tactic.

**Legal standpoint**
- Terminology is grey — they should be talking about a consultation service rather than a dispensary/product.
- If their strategy is working, happy to copy most of it.

## Other telehealth brands to watch

These aren't direct medicinal cannabis competitors, but they're relevant for branding, positioning, and broader telehealth strategy.

- **Legal:** Australian company, but services many countries (excluding USA).

### Pilot
- Key brand under **Eucalyptus** brands.

### Hims and Hers
- Largest global telehealth players; leaders in branding in the USA.

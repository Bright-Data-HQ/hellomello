Compliance

The guardrails on all of it

No claim that a product or cannabis treats, cures or helps any condition. Symptom and condition pages stay general and helpful, never tied to a product. Restricted topics are written as plain health education and reviewed by your named doctor before they go live. Nothing publishes without your sign-off.

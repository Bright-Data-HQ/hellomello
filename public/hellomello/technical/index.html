03 / The root causes

Most of the long list comes from four things.

A scan like this throws up thirty-odd warnings, which sounds like a lot. But once you group them, they trace back to just four causes. Fix the cause and a whole row of warnings disappears at once.

Cause 01

The "Home" menu link

An old version of your homepage still exists and bounces visitors to the real one. Trouble is, the "Home" button in your menu points at that old page, and the menu sits on every page. So that little detour is repeated site-wide.

The fix: repoint the "Home" link to the real homepage and retire the old page. It needs doing carefully across the templates so it holds on every page.
Cause 02

Old leftover pages

A few retired and duplicate pages were never fully removed. They're still published and still in your site map, so Google keeps tripping over them and flagging things like "orphan page" and "duplicate".

The fix: work through the retired pages one by one, deleting or redirecting each to the right place.
Cause 03

Wix builds heavy pages

Wix tends to produce very large, code-heavy pages. Your homepage is big enough to go over Google's reading limit, which can mean part of the page doesn't get read. It also slows the first moment of load.

The work: trim apps and widgets on the homepage to get it under the limit. This is involved, and likely needs a Wix specialist.
Cause 04

Thin labels and one broken label

The titles, descriptions and the hidden "business card" data that Google reads were never fully filled in, and the one bit you do have has an invalid value, so it doesn't count.

The work: rewrite every title and description, and fix the one invalid value. There's a fair bit here, page by page.

08 / Speed and weight

Fast enough for people, heavy for Google.

There's a nice distinction here that's worth understanding, because it changes how worried you should be.

The speed real people feel is fine. Google measures the actual experience of your visitors, and on that score you pass on both mobile and desktop. That's the number that affects your ranking, and it's green.

But the pages are heavier than they should be. This is a Wix thing more than anything you did. The homepage is big enough to go over the limit Google sets for reading a page, which risks part of it not being read. A few other pages are on the heavy side too.

The fix is to trim some of the apps, widgets and embedded bits on the homepage to bring the weight down. It's an involved job, likely needing a Wix specialist, but it helps both the reading limit and the speed at the same time, so it earns its place on the list.

09 / Glossary

The jargon, in plain English.

A few terms come up a lot in SEO. Here's what they actually mean, no fluff.

Redirect
An automatic forward from one web address to another. Handy when a page moves, but a link that points at a redirect makes everyone take a small detour they don't need.
Site map
A list of your pages that you hand to Google so it knows what to look at. It should only list real, current pages, not retired ones.
Orphan page
A page that nothing else links to. Visitors and Google can't easily find it, so it just floats there causing confusion.
Indexed
A page is "indexed" when Google has it in its records and can show it in search. Pages you don't want found, like a login screen, are deliberately kept out.
Structured data
Hidden labels on a page that spell out what it is, like a business card for Google. Done well, it helps you show up richer in search and get quoted by AI tools.
Title and description
The headline and the short blurb Google shows for your page in search results. The first thing people read before they decide to click.
Alt text
A short written description of an image. It helps people using screen readers, and it helps your images turn up in image search.
Page weight
How much data a page is made of. Heavier pages take longer to load and, past a point, Google may not read all of them.
